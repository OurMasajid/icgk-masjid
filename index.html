About Our Community

The Islamic Community of Greater Killeen (ICGK) is a welcoming masjid serving the Muslims of Killeen, Harker Heights, Copperas Cove, and the Fort Hood area.

We are a place of prayer, learning, and brotherhood and sisterhood. Whether you are a lifelong resident, new to the area, a soldier stationed nearby, or simply curious about Islam, our doors are open to you. We gather for the five daily prayers, Jumu'ah, Ramadan and the two Eids, and year-round programs for every age.

Our community is growing — and we are excited to expand our masjid to better serve everyone. We invite you to be part of it through your prayers, your presence, and your support.
