📖 What Is Islam?

Islam is the religion of submitting to the One God — Allah — in peace and sincerity. The word Islam itself comes from the Arabic root meaning both "peace" and "submission": an inner peace that comes from devoting one's life to the Creator.

A person who follows Islam is called a Muslim — "one who submits to God." Muslims believe in the same One God worshipped by the Prophets Adam, Noah, Abraham, Moses, and Jesus (peace be upon them all), and that Islam is the completion of that same eternal message.

Islam is not a new or foreign faith, but the final chapter of God's guidance to humanity, sent through His last Prophet, Muhammad ﷺ, in the 7th century.

☝️ Who Is Allah?

Allah is simply the Arabic word for God — the same word used by Arabic-speaking Jews and Christians. Muslims believe Allah is the One, Eternal Creator of everything: with no partner, no son, no equal, and nothing like Him.

"Say, He is Allah, the One. Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born, and there is none comparable to Him."— Qur'an 112 (Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ)

He is the Most Merciful and the Most Compassionate — closer to us than our own jugular vein — and it is to Him alone that worship is directed.

🌟 The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

Muslims believe that Allah sent Prophets to every nation to call people back to Him. The final Prophet was Muhammad ﷺ (peace be upon him), born in Makkah, who received the Qur'an over 23 years.

He was known even before prophethood as al-Amīn — "the Trustworthy." His life — his honesty, mercy, justice, and worship — is the living example (Sunnah) that Muslims strive to follow.

Muslims honor all the Prophets before him — including Jesus (ʿĪsā) and Moses (Mūsā), peace be upon them — and consider belief in them part of the faith.

💎 The Six Articles of Faith

At the heart of Islam are six core beliefs that every Muslim holds:

  • Belief in Allah — the One God, with no partner.
  • Belief in His Angels — created from light, who serve God.
  • Belief in His Books — including the Torah, the Gospel, and the final revelation, the Qur'an.
  • Belief in His Prophets — from Adam to Muhammad ﷺ, peace be upon them all.
  • Belief in the Day of Judgment — when all will be raised and held accountable.
  • Belief in Divine Decree — that nothing happens except by God's knowledge and will.

☝️ The Shahādah

Becoming a Muslim begins with a single, sincere testimony of faith:

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ ٱللَّٰهِ

Lā ilāha illā Allāh, Muḥammadun rasūlu Allāh

"I testify that there's no deity worthy of worship except Allah alone, and I testify that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah."
